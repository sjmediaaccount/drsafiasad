Stomach Burning (Acidity / Heartburn)

Common causes: spicy/junk food, empty-stomach tea or coffee, oily food, cold drinks, stress, dehydration, late meals. Simple relief: drink more water, regular meal timing, less tea/coffee, walk after meals, manage stress.
